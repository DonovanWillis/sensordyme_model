      CH.03 — Live signals
      
Blink-proof by design.

      
Every threshold is grounded in human-factors research — normal blinks,
        mirror checks, and mounted GPS units never alert. The instrument only speaks
        when the pattern is real.

    

    

      

        
Eye aspect ratio · 9s0.31

        
        
Closures under 500 ms are blinks — ignored entirely.
          0.5–1.5 s → microsleep warning. Over 1.5 s → drowsiness, loud tier.

      

      

        
PERCLOS · rolling 60s8.0%

        
        
Percent of time eyes closed. Over 15% → fatigue.
          Over 25% → critical — catches the drowsy-but-blinking driver.

      

      

        
Glance budget · 12s window0.0s

        
        
Cumulative eyes-off-road beyond 4.5 s in any 12 s fires
          even when no single glance crossed 2.0 s — the texting pattern.

      

      

        
Risk fusion · EMA12%

        
        
Eye, gaze, and object risk are weighted and corroborated —
          mildly drowsy and glancing away scores worse than either alone.

      

    

  


  
  

    

      
      

        CH.04 — The edge
      

      

        

          
Everything happens here.

          
Capture, inference, fusion, alerting — the whole pipeline runs on the
            device on your dash. No cloud. No account. No round trip.

        

        

          
0 Btransmitted · ever

          
Frames exist only in memory, for one loop iteration, then they're gone.
            Only derived events — a timestamp, a type, a confidence — touch the disk.

        

        

          
Enforced in code, not policy.
